# Customer Birthday Reminder

Never miss a customer birthday. The app reads your customer list, shows who has a birthday in the next 3 days, and gives you a ready-to-send wish in English, Bahasa Melayu or 中文 with a card image to paste into WhatsApp or email.

**Your customer data never leaves your phone or laptop.** The list is read and stored in your own browser. This site holds only the app, with no customer data.

## Install on your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the app link, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the app link, tap **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

It then opens like a normal app and works offline.

## How to use it
1. **Import your list once:** Customers tab → Choose file (Excel .xlsx or CSV). Columns: Customer Name, Company, Birthday, Phone, Email, Account Manager (Language is optional). Check the preview, then tap Import. Need a starting file? Tap "Get blank Excel template".
2. **Check daily:** the Upcoming tab lists everyone with a birthday in the next 3 days.
3. **Send a wish:** tap a customer, pick the language and card style, tap **Copy message**, then **Open chat** (WhatsApp) or copy the email. Save or share the card image and attach it.
4. **Mark as sent** so nobody sends it twice. The app never sends anything by itself.
5. **Monthly:** Monthly list tab → choose the month → Download PDF (summary plus one page per customer with the card and messages).
6. **Phone reminders:** Customers tab → "Add reminders to my calendar (.ics)". This adds an alert 3 days before each birthday and on the 1st of each month.

## Messy data
Unclear dates (such as 05/06), impossible or missing birthdays, phone numbers missing a leading 0, bad emails and duplicate rows are flagged for you to fix, never guessed. You confirm before the app replaces your list, saves an edit or deletes anything.

## Notes
- Data is kept per device and per browser. Keep your Excel file as the master copy and re-import it when it changes.
- Reading customer lists from PDF or photos works only in the Claude version of the app. Here, use Excel or CSV.
